# Subscription billing — how it works

This explains how paying for FlowyBookings works today, in plain terms.

## Plans

- **Trial** — 14 days free, full access, no card required upfront.
- **Starter, Pro, Premium** — paid plans, each unlocking more features (staff limits, SMS reminders, analytics, branding, etc).
- Prices can be **monthly** or **yearly**. Yearly costs 10× the monthly price (2 months free compared to paying monthly all year).

## Upgrading (a bigger plan, or switching to yearly)

- Takes effect **immediately** — you get the new features right away.
- You're charged the **full price** for the new plan right away, through Mollie's secure checkout page.
- Before anything happens, you'll always see a confirmation showing exactly what's changing (e.g. "Starter (Monthly) → Pro (Monthly)") and that you're about to be sent to Mollie to pay.

## Downgrading (a smaller plan, or switching to monthly)

- Does **not** happen immediately. You keep everything you already paid for until the end of your current billing period.
- On that date, your plan switches automatically — no action needed, no extra charge, no refund for the switch itself.
- You can change your mind any time before that date — there's a **"Keep current plan"** option that cancels the scheduled switch.
- Choosing a different downgrade target before it applies just updates what you'll switch to — no extra charges either way.

## Cancelling your subscription

- Same idea as downgrading: you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.
- After that, your account moves to a locked state — see below.

## If a payment fails

- You get **7 days** to update your payment method. Nothing is blocked immediately.
- If it's still not fixed after 7 days, new bookings are blocked until it's resolved. Your existing data (customers, past bookings, etc) is never affected.

## If your subscription lapses (nothing active, nothing scheduled)

This covers: your trial ran out with no plan chosen, your subscription was cancelled and the paid period ended, or a failed payment passed the 7-day grace window.

- You're signed out of the working dashboard and shown the plans page instead — pick a plan to get back in.
- Your data is never deleted or hidden — it's all still there once you resubscribe.
- The sidebar shows a lock icon on anything you can't access until you resubscribe.
- If you had a paid plan before, the plans page shows it as your **"Previous plan"** so it's a one-click resubscribe, not a hunt for what you used to have.

## Every change is confirmed first

Before any plan or billing-cycle change goes through, you'll see a clear summary of exactly what's changing and what happens next — including a heads-up whenever you're about to be redirected to Mollie's payment page. Nothing happens from a single click with no confirmation.

## Payments

All payments are processed securely by **Mollie**. FlowyBookings never stores your card or bank details directly.
